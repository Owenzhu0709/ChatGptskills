# UKNOW Knowledge Management

1. Identify the knowledge object, source, owner, audience, sensitivity, and freshness requirement.
2. Separate raw sources, interpretations, decisions, and reusable outputs.
3. Preserve provenance and link duplicates to one canonical source.
4. Define naming, retrieval, review, expiry, and archival rules.
5. Never promote unverified conversation claims to authoritative knowledge.

# UNUO Monetization

1. Identify payer, purchase trigger, delivered value, and measurable outcome.
2. Compare pricing and packaging options against user trust and product incentives.
3. Define entitlements, limits, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and exception handling.
4. State unit-economics assumptions and sensitivity ranges.
5. Propose a reversible validation experiment before irreversible billing complexity.

# UNUO Business Model

1. Start from verified customer and product context.
2. Map beneficiary, user, buyer, payer, and partner separately.
3. State the value exchanged, acquisition path, cost drivers, revenue mechanism, and critical assumptions.
4. Test incentives and failure modes for every participant.
5. Recommend experiments with decision thresholds; do not present estimates as validated facts.
